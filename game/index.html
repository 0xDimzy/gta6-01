ON FOOT WASD / Arrows — move  ·  Shift — sprint (drains stamina) · double-tap W — sprint without Shift
Space — jump  ·  G — dive roll (slips under traffic)  ·  C — crouch (heat cools twice as fast)
V — shove a pedestrian  ·  E — enter a car, grab a package / passenger / yacht papers
DRIVING W / S — accelerate / brake  ·  A / D — steer  ·  Space — handbrake (drift!)
Shift — nitro (FOV kicks wider)  ·  H — horn  ·  B — convertible roof up / down
E — exit the car (or board the speedboat from the pier)
WEAPONS F — take a weapon / ammo  ·  Click — shoot  ·  Q — switch weapon  ·  R — reload / retry mission
X — sniper scope (2.5x zoom)  ·  ammo boxes restock all three guns
Pistol downtown · shotgun by the park · sniper rifle in the old-town plaza · SMG east of the park.
HEAT — THE WANTED STARS Stars rise when the cops see you steal, shoot, or speed past them. Break line of sight and the blue HEAT COOLING bar fills — one star drops when it is full. Crouch out of sight and it cools twice as fast.
3 stars: roadblocks seal the street (blast through one at speed for +$100).
4 stars: backup arrives — a helicopter circles with a searchlight, and spike strips shred your tyres (top speed halved for 6 s).
5 stars: the SWAT van rolls — it rams for 14 damage and never backs off. A K9 unit may run you down on foot.
Get busted and the cops take $150. Finish a job at full health for a +$50 clean-getaway bonus; beat a timed leg with 30+ s to spare for +$150.
VEHICLES Yellow coupe downtown · red sports car east · white van by the park · pink convertible by the beach · teal motorcycle downtown · blue pickup north · speedboat moored in the harbor (board it with E from the pier).
A city bus loops the ring road and a yellow taxi cruises the grid — hail it with T ($20 a ride, it drives you to the beacon).
CAMERA, RADIO & WORLD Drag mouse — look around  ·  P — photo mode (freezes time, orbit the camera)  ·  O — drone camera (bird's-eye orbit)
N — minimap zoom (×1 / ×2 / ×4)  ·  Y — car radio: BAY FM synthwave, late jazz, or news
M — music on / off  ·  U — rain  ·  I — day / dusk  ·  Esc — pause
GAMEPAD Left stick moves / drives · right stick looks · A enter & shoot · B exit · X weapon · Y radio · LB / RB switch weapon · LT brake · RT nitro · Start pause · Back help. Triggers are analog; sticks have dead zones so a worn pad does not drift.
TIPS Follow the yellow beacon — it marks your next job. Missions run in order: First Wheels → Package Run → Heat Escape → Getaway Driver → Harbor Heist → Airport Getaway (the runway and tower sit on the west margin). A failed job pays half when you retry it. The red arrow at the edge of the screen points at whatever just hit you — and the ring around the minimap fills red with your wanted level.
